## Style: Grove

A quiet, editorial look inspired by a well-bound art monograph:

- **Colours:** deep forest green, warm parchment, and a single terracotta accent
- **Type:** Playfair Display for headlines (italic terracotta for emphasis), Jost for body text, JetBrains Mono for small labels
- **Details:** thin hairline rules, paintings mounted on parchment mats, and large faint numerals in the background

Each slide's accent underline is drawn as a hand-painted brushstroke, a nod to Fechin's own technique.

## Interactive effects

| Effect | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Cursor circle** | Every slide | A ring trails your mouse and grows when it passes over a painting |
| **Zoom on paintings** | Any painting | Click a painting to see it full screen; click again or press `Esc` to close |
| **Close-up / step back** | Slide 8 | Opens zoomed in on the brushstrokes around the eyes; click to pull back to the full portrait |
| **Animated journey map** | Slide 4 | His route draws itself city by city: Kazan → St. Petersburg → New York → Taos → Santa Monica → home to Kazan |
| **3D hover cards** | Slides 2, 6, 11, 12 | Boxes lift, tilt toward your cursor and show a terracotta border |
| **Brushstroke reveals** | Every slide | The accent stroke paints itself in as each slide opens |
| **Staggered entrances** | Every slide | Text and images fade up in sequence |

The cursor and hover effects need a mouse; on phones and tablets the deck works by swiping.
